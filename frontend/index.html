How it works

Three moves. Every one sealed.

01 · Shield
Into the vault

Park a balance in the vault. Your open wallet stops showing that bag.

02 · Trade
Size sealed

Trade tokenized stocks and crypto. The market never sees how much you moved.

03 · Move
Send or cash out

Pay to a tag or cash out. A vault proof settles, never how much.

What settles in public. What stays sealed.

Your size
Off the open book

On-chain min-out is a floor, not your real amount. The explorer sees a proof and an asset pair, never how much you moved.

Your balance
Visible only to you

Shielded notes hold your position. A viewing key proves balance to an auditor without revealing your history to everyone else.

Your strategy
Privacy by protocol design

The target design separates user activity through cryptographic proofs. Actual unlinkability depends on the deployed circuit, contracts, and execution path.

The oldest idea in markets

A dark pool for everything onchain.

Dark pools let large players trade without showing their hand. RobTrade brings that to tokenized stocks, crypto, and stablecoin payments, sealed by a proof and settled on Robinhood Chain and Tempo.

Reveal nothing

Settlement is public. Strategy is not.

Anyone can verify that your vault settled. No one can see what you hold, what you traded, or how much you moved.
